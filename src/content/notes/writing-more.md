[Beat](https://beathagenlocher.com/) convinced me to write more by lowering the
bar and instead of creating fully fledged well thought through blogposts,
document notes and thoughts more frequently as they occur and revise them when
they are no longer correct or up to date. This is also known as _evergreen
notes_ i.e. notes where the emphasis is not on the chronological order (as a
blog traditionally does) but notes that are mainly optimized for myself and
meant to be updated (or even deleted) whenever appropriate. Let's make this
happen!

Somes notes based on Beat's recommendations why and how to write filtered
through my unreliable memory and prioritization (meaning he might not endorse
this as his recommendations, at least not the most important ones):

- Prefer writing under your real name since it's more uncommon for people to
  become popular with pseudonyms (at least Beat claimed this).
- Register a domain e.g. NAMESURNAME.com for example on cloudflare.com (no
  affiliation).
- Use a static site generator like [Astro](https://astro.build) instead of
  something like Wordpress.
- Find some examples of personal websites you like and tell Claude via Claude
  Design to create a few examples of similarly styled pages for yourself. Then
  choose one and use Claude to set up the whole website.
- Publish via GitHub Pages (Claude can tell you what do do).
- Find your own style of writing but consider writing small notes and thoughts
  on a ~daily basis instead of full-fledged complex blogposts that you never
  end up finishing. If you ever feel like it, it's easier to grab a bunch of
  notes on related topics and then writing an essay based on them.
- Such a personal website is valuable to have as a reference when applying for
  jobs and trying to demonstrate your proficiency. It's also very possible that
  few people will read it. It's still valuable to write for a potential
  audience instead of only writing private notes at home because it forces you
  to optimize for clarity, which is a valuable skill.
