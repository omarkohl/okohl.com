[Beat](https://beathagenlocher.com/) convinced me to write more by lowering the bar and instead of creating fully fledged well thought through blogposts, document notes and thoughts more frequently as they occur and revise them when they are no longer correct or up to date. This is also known as _evergreen notes_ i.e. notes where the emphasis is not on the chronological order (as a blog traditionally does) but notes that are mainly optimized for myself and meant to be updated (or even deleted) whenever appropriate. Let's make this happen!
